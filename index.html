About Rizza Court

A mall built around one shopping account

Rizza Court brings together fashion, electronics, beauty, dining and home goods under one roof — and one system. Every purchase, discount and receipt lives in your account, no matter which store on the floor you bought from.

🛍

Products offered

Apparel, footwear, electronics, beauty, home goods and dining — over 4,000 SKUs across 6 categories.

⚙

Services

Personal styling, gift wrapping, click-and-collect, and a concierge desk on Level 1.

🏬

Store branches

Three branches across Cebu City, Dalaguete, and Argao, each independently stocked.

👤

Customer experience

Browse, save favorites, track orders and re-order in a few taps from any device.

📊

Business information

Founded 2016. Family-owned, independently operated, 180+ tenant stores mall-wide.

🏷

Promotions

Rotating flash sales, seasonal markdowns and a members-only discount tier.
